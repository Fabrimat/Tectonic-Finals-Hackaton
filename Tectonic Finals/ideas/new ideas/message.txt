# Idea: Ripple

KBC predicts a customer's energy transition long before they ask, and connects them to the right Belgian partners at the right time. The customer confirms every step. Partners only ever see an anonymous, confirmed intent.

## Direction

- Ecosystem: the energy transition of buildings and mobility. One vertical, named Belgian partners, a clear role for each.
- Early prediction. The aim is to see an intent forming months or years ahead, not to react to an event that already happened. The strongest signals are dates that are known long in advance.
- Primary target: homeowners in Flanders, the largest base KBC can reach and the one that scales to millions of customers. It is also the only segment where the full ladder, from three years ahead to ready, can be shown.
- Second scenario: a small business with premises and vehicles. It shows that the same engine adapts its time scale to the segment, with a ladder compressed to six to twelve months.
- Home and car are examples of what the engine serves, not its scope.

## The problem

Customers notice a need late, compare options alone and coordinate several providers themselves. Energy is a clear case: insulation, heating, solar panels, batteries, charging for an electric vehicle, the energy contract, grants and financing come from different companies, with different rules and windows to act. Most people wait until a deadline or a breakdown forces them.

## Known dates make early prediction credible

- Renovation obligation in Flanders. A home with an EPC label E or F must reach at least label D within six years of a change of ownership. The clock starts on a date the bank already knows, because it financed the purchase. Renovating takes a year or more to plan, and most owners start late. (The period was five years before 2025 and the stricter later steps were dropped. Confirm the current text with the Flemish energy agency before the pitch.)
- Grant changes. Since 1 March 2026 the Mijn VerbouwPremie for wall, roof and floor insulation and for high-efficiency glazing has stopped for owner-occupiers in income categories 1 and 2, and heat pump amounts were lowered. The EPC label premium ends on 1 July 2026. Less subsidy means more need for financing and for well-timed group offers.
- Low-emission zones. Euro 5 diesel cars and vans have been banned in Brussels since 1 January 2026. Fine start dates are reported inconsistently, so we cite the ban and not the fines.
- Mortgage and loan maturity, age of the heating system, and consumption that keeps rising year after year.

Dates and amounts above must be rechecked against official sources before they appear in the demo.

## The intent ladder

Every customer moves from weak to strong signals. KBC behaves differently at each stage.

| Stage | Lead time | Example signals (household) | What KBC does |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whisper | 1 to 3 years | Home financed by KBC with a poor EPC label, renovation clock running, energy bill up year after year | Shows an insight in the customer's own view: "this is your consumption trend, no need to act, just be aware". Offers two optional actions: connect the meter data through Fluvius, and a small preparation step with a partner such as an EPC check. No product is offered, nothing is shared with partners without the customer's choice |
| Forming | 3 to 12 months | Heating system reaching end of life, visits to the energy pages in KBC Mobile, a repair invoice, a grant window closing | Asks the customer: "Are you planning to renovate?", with a checklist and typical costs |
| Ready | 0 to 3 months | Deposit or quote paid, boiler failure, contract in preparation | Builds the full plan across partners, which the customer approves step by step |

The customer opts in to an energy radar first. Without that consent nothing is analysed beyond what the customer already sees, and in the whisper stage nothing leaves the bank.

### Say more as certainty grows

KBC says very little when it is only guessing and more as the evidence firms up.

- Three years ahead: awareness only, plus the optional data connection and a small preparation step.
- About a year ahead: an opportunity appears, for example solar panels that fit the final plan.
- About six months ahead: KBC pushes more and connects partners such as a supplier, an electric vehicle with charging, or a bike.
- Ready: the full plan.

The customer should never feel watched. Messages are rare and never pushy, the customer is told which data is used, and a confirmation of that data is requested before anything is built on it. The customer stays in control at every stage.

### The ladder adapts to the segment

The same engine stretches or compresses the ladder. A household has years to prepare. A small business decides its energy and fleet transition within six to twelve months, and its life cycle is shorter, so its stages are shorter.

| Stage | Household | Small business |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whisper | 1 to 3 years | 6 to 12 months |
| Forming | 3 to 12 months | 3 to 6 months |
| Ready | 0 to 3 months | 0 to 3 months |

### Context from the area

Signals combine personal data with data about the area: the age of the building stock around the customer, whether the home is in a historic city centre where rules are stricter, and which group purchase rounds are open nearby. The plan changes with the place, for example a different approach to insulation or panels in a protected centre than in a recent suburb. Area rules must be verified before they are stated in the demo.

## The levers

1. Timing: tell the customer when to act, for example before a grant changes, when a group purchase round opens, or well before the renovation deadline.
2. Preparation: EPC data, documents and estimates already filled in, so the next step is cheap.
3. Introduction: a warm introduction to the right partner, carried by an anonymous confirmed intent.
4. Financing: a loan sized against the expected savings.

Every plan also carries at least one "not needed" card, based on what the customer already has. That is what keeps the advice credible.

## The ecosystem

KBC is the orchestrator. Each partner has one role.

| Role | Partner | What it brings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Data | Fluvius | Meter and consumption data, shared only with consent |
| Certificates | De Immowinkel | EPC certificates and inspections |
| Advice and grants | Setle, Encon | Renovation cost and grant estimates, sustainability advice |
| Installation | Impact us today, plus a solar and battery installer to choose | Insulation, heating, air conditioning, panels, batteries |
| Charging | Blink Charging (formerly Blue Corner) | Home and business charging for electric vehicles |
| Collective buying and supply | Samen Overstappen, provincial group purchases, winning suppliers such as EnergyVision and Mega | Group prices on electricity, gas and solar panels |
| Business footprint | KBC business CO2 insights, Kube ESG, Sweco | Emissions insight, ESG reporting, water resilience |
| Finance and protection | KBC | Energy loan, home insurance, electric car insurance, leasing |

Partners are assumptions for the demo, not agreements. Setle, Encon, Impact us today, De Immowinkel, Sweco and Kube ESG are already in the KBC catalogue. Group purchases of electricity and gas through Samen Overstappen are already offered inside KBC Mobile.

Value flows: KBC sends qualified, confirmed demand to partners and earns commission and retention. Partners get leads with no acquisition cost. The customer gets the right plan at the right time and lower running costs.

## Group purchases already exist

Belgium already has group purchases (groepsaankoop) for electricity, gas, solar panels and heat pumps, run by provinces, energy cooperatives and platforms such as Samen Overstappen. We do not rebuild them. Where one is open and fits the intent, Ripple routes the customer to it. What those programmes lack is timing: they open in fixed windows customers miss, and they do not know when a specific household is ready.

## What the customer sees

1. Predict. Rules over trends, known dates and spending place the customer on the ladder.
2. Confirm. A short card says what we think is coming and which data we used. Nothing is computed or shared until the customer says yes.
3. Plan. One board of steps across the partners above. Each step shows why it appears and can be accepted, deferred or rejected.
4. Act. Accepted steps become anonymous intents for the right partner: service, area, timeframe, budget band, never a name.
5. Learn. The partner view aggregates confirmed demand per area, only above a minimum group size.

## Why it fits the challenge

- Recognises an emerging need before the customer does, from deterministic signals and known dates.
- The customer stays in control at every stage.
- A vertical ecosystem with named Belgian partners and a clear role for each.
- Triple win: the customer lowers running costs and avoids deadline stress, KBC earns commission and keeps the customer, partners get qualified demand.
- Scalable: detection is rules over transaction categories, trends and dates, cheap and auditable. A language model only writes the plan from a short list of catalogue entries, once per confirmed intent.
- One engine for households and businesses.

## Scenarios for the demo

- Household: a couple in Ghent who bought a 1960s terraced house with a KBC mortgage. EPC label E, so the six-year renovation clock started at purchase. Whisper: the deadline and a rising energy bill. Forming: a 22-year-old boiler and visits to the energy pages. Ready: a repair invoice for the boiler. The plan covers certificate, cost and grant estimate, insulation, heat pump, solar, group purchase of the energy contract, financing and an insurance update. One card says a home battery is not needed yet.
- Business: a bakery with six employees in Leuven, an older building with energy-hungry ovens and two diesel delivery vans that serve Brussels. The ladder is compressed to about a year. Whisper (6 to 12 months): energy costs rising against revenue. Forming (3 to 6 months): the Brussels ban reaches the vans. Ready: a deposit on an electric van. The plan covers CO2 insight and sector comparison, sustainability advice, solar for the roof, charging, van financing, and an energy contract. One card says an ESG report is not needed at this size.

Same engine, different signals.

## Screens

- Customer app, mobile: radar and stage, confirm card, plan board, summary with what each partner receives.
- Partner portal: anonymous leads and aggregated demand per area, with a panel stating what the partner does not see.
- Ecosystem map: KBC in the centre, partners by role, and the flows between them.
- Numbers page: customer saving, KBC revenue and retention, partner leads. All assumptions sit in one visible table and are marked as estimates.

## Pitch outline (3 minutes)

1. One household, and a deadline that started three years ago without anyone noticing.
2. The ladder: whisper, forming, ready.
3. The confirm card, then the plan spreading across the ecosystem.
4. The customer rejects one step, defers one, and one card says "not needed".
5. The ecosystem map and the partner screen.
6. The numbers page.
7. Ten seconds on the bakery: same engine, different signals.
8. Close: KBC stops being the shop and becomes the customer's agent for the transition.

## Constraints and risks

- Synthetic data only, and we say so.
- Predicting years ahead can feel intrusive. The energy radar is opt-in and the whisper stage stays inside the customer's own view.
- Regulations, grants and group purchase rounds change. We verify them before the pitch and present partners as examples, not agreements.
- The model may only pick catalogue entries, and every returned id is checked against the catalogue.
- Scenarios are precomputed and cached, so the demo does not depend on a live model call on the organisers' device.
- Aggregated demand needs consent. We state the minimum group size and the area level on the slide.
- Energy is the most popular domain. We stand apart through early prediction from known dates, integration with existing group purchases instead of rebuilding them, and the honest "not needed" card.
- Scope freezes at 13:30, then fixes, security findings and the recorded fallback video.
