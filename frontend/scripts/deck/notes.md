# Speaker notes

About 20 seconds per slide, three minutes in total. Lines starting with "To check" are for the speaker only and never appear on a slide.

## 1. Title

Most people notice an energy need late, when something breaks or a deadline arrives. We built a way for KBC to see it coming and to help before the customer has to ask. Everything you will see is synthetic data and simulated integrations.

## 2. A deadline nobody noticed

Meet a couple in Ghent. They buy a 1960s terraced house with a KBC mortgage. The energy label is E, so a renovation obligation starts a clock on the day they buy. Nobody tells them, the years pass, and most owners start late. Grants have also changed, so waiting gets more expensive.

To check: the six year period and the label E or F to D rule, and the grant changes since 1 March 2026, against the Flemish energy agency pages.

## 3. What KBC already does, and the gap

KBC already offers solar quotes, installation tracking and group purchases of energy. We build on those. What is missing is the early part: knowing years ahead that a household will need to act, and one confirmed plan across the partners.

To check: each existing KBC service named here on the KBC site before the pitch.

## 4. The intent ladder

Three stages. Whisper, one to three years ahead: awareness only, inside the customer's own view, nothing shared. Forming, a few months ahead: one simple question. Ready: a plan. KBC says very little when it is guessing and more as the evidence firms up.

## 5. Four consents

Consent is not one switch. Analysis, goal, sharing and commitment are each asked on their own and can be withdrawn. A partner first sees an anonymous intent, never a name. Contact details follow only when the customer authorises that partner for that step. This is also our GDPR slide.

## 6. One plan across partners

One board, with a reason for every step. The customer accepts one, saves one for later, says no to one. And a card says "not needed", for example a home battery before panels. That honesty is what makes the advice credible. The partners are examples, not agreements.

## 7. The ecosystem

KBC sits in the centre and each partner has one role: data, certificates, advice, installation, charging, collective buying. Existing group purchases are plugged in rather than rebuilt, and the engine adds the timing they lack. All partner names are demo assumptions.

## 8. Value for KBC and its partners

KBC values an ecosystem first by conversion into its own products: the energy loan, insurance, leasing. That is the big number, a demo estimate per customer. Partner commission comes second and only exists for some partners. Advisors see anonymous leads and demand per area. All assumptions are in one visible table.

## 9. Same engine, different signals, and close

The same engine for a bakery in Leuven with ovens and two diesel vans: the ladder shrinks to about a year, the signals change, the principle does not. KBC stops being only the shop and becomes the customer's agent for the transition. Thank you.

To check: the Brussels low-emission zone rule for older diesel vans, and its start date, before it is spoken.
